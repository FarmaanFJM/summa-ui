# Candidate Summa-UI Web rules W01 to W10 (DRAFT, not approved)

Full text: `docs/SUMMA_WEB.md` in this repository (sections W1 to W10 and the brand lockup). Machine form: `principles.json` here. Evidence: FB-0052 to FB-0057. Nothing in `guidance/` or `PRINCIPLES.md` changed.

## W01 Structured Asymmetry

One column grid, deliberately unequal compositions: cards differ in width, height, weight and surface, edges stay on the grid, a feature insert lets content flow beside it and reclaim the width beneath it.

- Asymmetry is intentional, never random gaps.
- Not a regular grid with unequal cards: use row spanning so content flows around an insert.
- Do not reserve an empty column for a small insert.
- Keep a sensible reading order and a deliberate mobile order.
- Never in a primary object workspace or dense tool.

## W02 Rectangular Surfaces

Square or subtly rounded corners, purpose built cards, hierarchy by surface step, type and whitespace.

- 2px to 8px corners; no pill containers.
- One card design per kind of content, not one generic card.
- Share behaviour, allow compositional difference; avoid components with dozens of flags.

## W03 Charcoal, Emerald and Warm

Charcoal is the foundation, emerald means action and progress, a warm palette means editorial, educational and historical material and featured surfaces.

- Emerald and warm are not interchangeable.
- Not every card is coloured; a warm surface is an insert, never the page.
- Text on warm is ink; focus on warm is a dark ring.
- Needs an explicit decision against P06.

## W04 Web Typography

Two self hosted faces and a scale with roles: display, title, section, card, body, support, metadata, eyebrow, editorial.

- Functional sans for navigation, controls, tools and body.
- Editorial serif for the wordmark, editorial headings, features and educational or historical content, never in a dense tool.
- No third party font fetch; few families and weights.

## W05 Action Hierarchy

Actions look like what they are: primary, secondary, tertiary, ink, outline, icon only, destructive, branded integration.

- One dominant primary per region.
- Tertiary is a quiet text action with an arrow.
- Ink is the solid dark action for light or warm surfaces.
- Not every action is emerald or a charcoal rectangle with a chevron.

## W06 Restrained Focus and Hover

Subtle hover, focus-visible rings of 1px to 1.5px at 3:1 against the surface, a dark ring on warm surfaces.

- Pointer interaction never draws a large ring.
- No thick or emerald rings, glows, expanding borders or nested outlines.
- Validate focus contrast on charcoal and on warm surfaces.

## W07 Selection Without a Stripe

A selected navigation item or card is a surface step and heavier type, with no coloured left stripe.

- Settles the P03 reading for selection.

## W08 Third Party Identity

A third party entry may use a light surface and its mark where terms permit, without decorative labels or claims of partnership.

- No label that only says third party.
- No wording implying partnership or endorsement without one.
- The mark is shown as supplied.

## W09 Real Imagery

Imagery is meaningful to the content; nothing is fabricated to fill an empty state.

- No unrelated stock photography.
- Empty states are deliberate and consistent.

## W10 Deliberate Responsive Composition

Desktop, tablet and mobile are designed, not collapsed.

- Main content before supporting features on mobile where it serves the task.
- Local navigation becomes a compact selector.
- No horizontal overflow; touch targets follow the approved rule.
